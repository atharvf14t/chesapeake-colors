How it works

You pick the look. We do the rest.

  1. 01

    Free design consult

    A walk-around or a few photos is all we need. You get a quote and a mockup of your home lit up.

  2. 02

    Custom-cut to your roofline

    We measure every run, then cut commercial C9 line to length. No coiled extra, no dangling cords. Pick warm white, pure white or color.

  3. 03

    Install day

    An insured crew of 2–4 with standoff ladders and fall protection. We use clip-only mounting and GFCI power, set the timer, then do a final walk-through at dusk.

  4. 04

    All-season care

    Wind, ice, a squirrel with opinions. Text us and we're out within 48 hours at no charge.

  5. 05

    Takedown & storage

    Starting January 1 we take it all down, label it by roofline and store it climate-controlled. Next year it goes back up faster.
